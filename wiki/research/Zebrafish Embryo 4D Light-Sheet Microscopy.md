## For future agent
This research synthesis documents the light-sheet fluorescence microscopy imaging characteristics of zebrafish embryos during early development (epiboly, gastrulation, and organogenesis). High temporal frequency (sub-minute frames) combined with anisotropic axial voxel pitch requires dedicated physical coordinate scaling and curvature-aware motion models.

---

## Imaging Characteristics
- Modality: Multi-view Light-Sheet Fluorescence Microscopy (LSFM).
- Contrast Channel: Nuclear-localized fluorescent protein markers (e.g. H2B-GFP).
- Voxel Pitch: Anisotropic ($Z=1.625\,\mu\text{m}$, $YX=0.40625\,\mu\text{m}$).
- Biological Motion: Coordinated morphogenetic streaming flows and cell divisions (mitosis).
